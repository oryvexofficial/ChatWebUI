Privacy and trust

Know where your traffic goes.

Each route works differently. Your choices decide what leaves your device and where it terminates.

This site

No accounts, telemetry, analytics, or external fonts. Every asset is bundled locally. Links to GitHub leave this site.

Fox

An unofficial client for eligible Firefox Accounts. Mozilla handles account authentication and service access under its own terms.

Your relay

If you deploy a Cloudflare Worker relay, Fox sends control-plane requests through your own Cloudflare account. General VPN traffic does not use it.

Tokens and settings

The Cloudflare API token is used only during Worker deployment and is never saved by OryvexVPN. Fox keeps the relay URL and secret locally on your device.

Route differences

Tor, WireGuard, MASQUE, Fox and Bypass differ in threat model. Bypass is a direct route and does not change your public IP address.

Open source

Inspect the Rust core, Go Fox service, Flutter UI, and Cloudflare Worker allowlist directly in the repository.
