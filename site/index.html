03 · Fox relay

Your own relay on Cloudflare.

When Mozilla control-plane endpoints are hard to reach, deploy a personal Cloudflare Worker directly from Fox settings. OryvexVPN uploads the audited Worker script, generates a 40-character secret, and configures Fox automatically.

  1. Deploy opens the guided setup modal inside OryvexVPN.
  2. Token Template opens Cloudflare with the exact Worker permissions pre-selected.
  3. The app verifies your account, uploads the Worker, enables workers.dev, and saves the relay URL + secret into Fox.

The relay carries Fox account and control-plane requests only, not general VPN traffic. Use your own Cloudflare account.

06 · FAQ

Short answers.

What happens if I click Connect on Fox without an account?

OryvexVPN immediately opens the custom Account Required for Fox modal so you can create or sign in with Mozilla (or via Email & Password) before starting the tunnel.

Is the Cloudflare relay a general VPN?

No. It forwards Fox control-plane requests such as account authentication and server list updates. General tunnel traffic does not pass through the Worker.

Where does my Cloudflare API token go?

It is used once to deploy the Worker to your Cloudflare account and is never saved in OryvexVPN settings. Fox stores only the resulting relay URL and random secret locally.

Is Fox an official Mozilla product?

No. Fox is an unofficial client and is not made, endorsed or supported by Mozilla.
